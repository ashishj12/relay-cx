### 3. Moments that felt wrong

- Turn 3: “Gemini is currently rate limited.” I don't know what Gemini is or when I should try again.
- Turns 3–6 and 9: “Your request has been saved for review.” I didn't see a reference number, who would review it, how I would hear back or how long it might take.
- Turn 5: I never reached a summary or approval step for roaming, so I couldn't judge whether I understood the change.
- Turn 6: “Request human support” led to the same failure as the normal questions. This felt like a dead end.
- Turn 9: The app didn't clearly say that booking taxis was outside its job.
- Before the conversation: “A router identifies the right specialist” and “authenticated account” felt like language for the people building the app.

It never asked me to repeat an answer, because it never got far enough to ask a follow-up question.

### 4. Moments that felt great

- The wrong-password message told me what to check.
- I could see my sample account and plan immediately after signing in.
- The page showed that it was working instead of silently ignoring my message.
- It refused the other account's bill.
- My conversation remained when I returned today.
- It didn't pretend that roaming had been added or my connection had been fixed.

### 5. Would I use this again?

**Only if desperate.** I couldn't get my bill, fix the connection or get a clear handoff to a person. Remembering my conversation helps, but I still don't know whether anyone will act on it.

### 6. Top 5 improvements

1. Let me ask for a person even when the assistant is having trouble.
2. Give me a reference number and explain how I can follow up; don't promise a reply time you don't know.
3. Use everyday words and tell me what I can do next when something fails.
4. Show the exact change, any price and what will stay unchanged before asking me to agree.
5. Let me return after signing in again without losing my conversation or an unfinished request.

### 7. Honesty check

I saw no claim that my service was fixed, roaming was added or a taxi was booked. I saw no other customer's bill. The 65.00 / month account card looked specific to the supplied Jordan account, but it was not an answer about last month's bill. “Saved for review” had no visible reference or follow-up details, so I couldn't confirm that promise from the conversation. I did not test a true next-day return. I did not read source code, settings, logs or execution details during this customer session.
